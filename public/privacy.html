Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Amour Privé may collect, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Effective date · 9 September 2026

1. Who we are

Amour Privé is a Nairobi-founded private travel curation house serving travellers and partners internationally. For privacy questions, contact amourprivetravel@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, destination preferences, travel dates, traveller details, enquiry messages, booking information, professional information when you contact us as a partner or curator, and information you voluntarily provide to us.

We may also receive technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from technical data, pages viewed and basic website activity. We do not intentionally collect payment-card information through ordinary website enquiry forms.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information to respond to enquiries, design and administer journeys, communicate about bookings, coordinate with relevant travel suppliers, manage partnerships and curator relationships, improve the website, maintain security, keep appropriate business records and comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal bases

Where applicable, we process personal information because it is necessary to provide a service or take steps at your request, because you have consented, because processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, or because the law requires it. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it subject to legal and operational limitations.

5. Travel suppliers and service providers

To arrange a journey, we may need to share relevant information with airlines, hotels, cruise operators, transport providers, guides, experience providers, payment or technology providers and other parties involved in delivering the requested service. We aim to share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. International transfers

Luxury travel is inherently international, so information may need to be processed in countries outside Kenya when we work with overseas suppliers or service providers. Where required, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards and protect personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

7. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to complete or document transactions, resolve disputes, maintain business records, prevent abuse, or satisfy legal and regulatory obligations. Retention periods vary according to the type and purpose of the information.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Cookies and local storage

The website may use essential browser technologies and local storage to remember preferences or support website functionality. Third-party services used for fonts, imagery, forms or other website functions may process technical information according to their own policies.

10. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, portability where applicable, and withdrawal of consent. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us at amourprivetravel@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

11. Marketing communications

We will only send promotional communications where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent. You can ask us to stop marketing communications at any time.

12. Children

Our website and services are directed at travellers and adults arranging travel. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information directly from children. Where information about a child is required for a legitimate travel booking, it should be provided by a parent, guardian or authorised adult.

13. Third-party websites

Our website may link to external websites, including travel suppliers and social platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their privacy notices before providing information to them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the current version took effect. Material changes may be highlighted through appropriate website or direct communication where required.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, email amourprivetravel@gmail.com. Amour Privé · Nairobi / Worldwide.
